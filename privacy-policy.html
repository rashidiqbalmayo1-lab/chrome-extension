Privacy Policy

Discogs to Shopify Lister Chrome Extension

Last Updated: May 22, 2026

Introduction

Discogs to Shopify Lister ("the Extension") is a free Chrome extension that helps record sellers import product listings from Discogs to Shopify. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

Data Stored Locally

The Extension stores the following information locally on your device using Chrome's storage API:

  • User Preferences: Your settings and configuration choices
  • Shopify Connection Details: Your Shopify store URL and API credentials (stored securely in your browser)
  • Discogs Product Data: Temporary cache of product information being transferred (title, description, price, images, condition)

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers
  • We do not track your browsing history
  • We do not share your data with third parties
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools
  • We do not sell your data

How We Use Your Information

The Extension uses your data solely to:

  1. Extract Product Information: Read product details from Discogs listings when you activate the extension
  2. Create Shopify Listings: Transfer product data to your Shopify store via the Shopify API
  3. Save Preferences: Remember your settings for a better user experience
  4. Maintain Connection: Keep your Shopify store connected for seamless imports

Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored locally in your Chrome browser using Chrome's secure storage API
  • Your Shopify API credentials are encrypted by Chrome's storage system
  • No data is transmitted to our servers or any third-party services except:
    • Product data sent directly to your Shopify store (as required for the extension to function)
    • Product information read from Discogs (publicly available listing data)

Third-Party Services

The Extension interacts with:

  • Discogs.com: To read publicly available product listing information
  • Your Shopify Store: To create and update product listings via the Shopify API

These services have their own privacy policies:

  • Discogs Privacy Policy: https://www.discogs.com/privacy
  • Shopify Privacy Policy: https://www.shopify.com/legal/privacy

Your Rights and Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: All data is stored locally and accessible through Chrome's developer tools
  • Delete: Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data
  • Modify: You can update or delete your Shopify connection details at any time within the extension
  • Control: You choose when to activate the extension and what listings to import

Permissions Explanation

The Extension requests the following permissions:

  • storage: To save your preferences and connection details locally
  • activeTab: To read product information from the Discogs page you're viewing (only when you click the extension icon)
  • Host Permissions: (discogs.com, your Shopify domain) To read Discogs listings and communicate with your Shopify store

Children's Privacy

The Extension is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top will reflect when changes were made. Continued use of the Extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Data Retention

Data is retained locally in your browser until you:

  • Clear the extension's data
  • Uninstall the extension
  • Clear your browser's storage

We do not retain any data on external servers.
